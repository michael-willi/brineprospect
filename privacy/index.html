Brine Prospect / Privacy

Privacy

Updated October 5, 2026.

What we collect

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist we store your email address, the role you select, the page you signed up on, and a one-line summary of what you last entered in the calculator.
  • Page views. We count visits to each page with the time, the site that referred you, whether you are on a phone or a computer, and whether it is your first visit. The count carries no name, email or IP address. A first-visit flag is saved in your browser so we can tell new visitors from returning ones.
  • Contact form. If you send a message we store your name, email address and the message so we can reply.
  • Calculator. Your inputs stay in your browser so they are there when you come back. They reach us only as the one-line summary attached to a waitlist signup.

What we do with it

We use waitlist emails to tell you when the test kit is available, and the rest to decide how many kits to stock and who they are for. We do not sell or share your information, and we do not send marketing from other companies.

Your choices

To leave the waitlist or have your information deleted, send us a message.
